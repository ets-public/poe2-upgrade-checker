Path of Exile 2 build planning and gear checks

Path of Building for PoE2, in game on every item.

Path of Building 2 is the build planner many Path of Exile 2 players use to work out their damage, effective HP and resistances. This page covers how to get your build into it, how to check gear and trade with it, and how to get its verdict on any item without leaving the game.

Get the free overlay Path of Building 2 on GitHub

What is Path of Building 2?

Path of Building Community makes two programs: the original for Path of Exile 1, and a separate one for Path of Exile 2, usually called Path of Building 2 or PoB2. Both are free and open source. You plan your passive tree, skills and gear in it, and it calculates what your character really does: damage per second for each skill, effective hit pool, resistances, Spirit and more.

Download PoB2 only from its official page, github.com/PathOfBuildingCommunity/PathOfBuilding-PoE2. It updates itself after that.

Getting your PoE2 build into Path of Building

  1. Your own character: open Import/Export Build, pick your realm and import the character from your account. Your tree, gems and gear come in as they are in game.
  2. A build someone shared: paste its build code or its pobb.in link in the same tab.
  3. Save it. Saved builds are plain files you can open again, share as a code, or upload to pobb.in.

Checking gear with Path of Building

To see if an item is better than what you wear, copy it in game (hover it and press Ctrl+C), then in PoB's Items tab click Create custom… and paste it. Hovering the new item shows what it would change in each slot it fits. It works, but every item means an alt-tab, a paste and a hover, which gets slow when you have several items to check.

Path of Building 2 also has a Trader (Trade for these items in the Items tab). It weighs each stat by what it does to your build and builds a weighted trade search for a slot. Logged in to pathofexile.com, it can search and compare listings without leaving Path of Building.

Path of Building's verdict in game, with one key

PoE Upgrade Checker is a free overlay that runs Path of Building's calculation engine on your build, so you don't have to alt-tab:

  • Hover an item and press F2. It's swapped into your build in the right slot and the popup shows what changes: damage, effective HP, resistances, Spirit, movement speed. Rings and jewels are tried in every slot they fit.
  • Clear verdicts: Upgrade, Sidegrade, Loses resistance, Loses damage, so an item that caps a resistance but costs you a fifth of your minion damage doesn't look like an upgrade.
  • Trade search with Path of Building's weights: Find upgrades uses the same weighted search as PoB's Trader, then judges every listing with your build and shows them in the app.
  • Where to spend: every slot searched in your price range and ranked by how much better your build gets per divine.
  • Your build stays in sync: import it once from pobb.in or a build code, or link the build file you save in Path of Building and the app follows each save.

You still plan your build in Path of Building. The overlay is for the moment you pick something up, or look at a listing, and want to know if it's better. It also does price checks, map and waystone checks and a league start route: see all features.

The overlay reads items with one Ctrl+C per key press, the same as copying an item yourself, and never reads game memory. It's a fan project, not affiliated with Grinding Gear Games or with the Path of Building Community team; it uses Path of Building's engine under its MIT licence.

Questions

Is there a Path of Building for Path of Exile 2?

Yes. Path of Building Community makes a separate version for Path of Exile 2, often called Path of Building 2 or PoB2. It's free and open source, and you download it from the project's GitHub page. The Path of Exile 1 version is a different program.

How do I get my PoE2 character into Path of Building?

In Path of Building 2, open Import/Export Build. Pick your realm and import your character from your account, or paste a build code or a pobb.in link someone shared.

Is PoE Upgrade Checker the same as Path of Building?

No. It's a separate free overlay that runs Path of Building's calculation engine (MIT licence) on your build, so you can get its verdict on an item in game without alt-tabbing. You still plan your build in Path of Building. It isn't made by or affiliated with the Path of Building Community team.

Do I need Path of Building installed to use the overlay?

No. The overlay brings its own copy of Path of Building's engine. You give it your build once: a pobb.in link, a Path of Building export code, or your saved build file, which it follows every time you save.

Stop alt-tabbing. Press F2.
