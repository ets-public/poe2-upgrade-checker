# Changelog

## Unreleased
- **Checking an item you have up for sale no longer prices it off your own listing:** the trade search finds your listing too (the same item, every mod at the same value), and its asking price was counted as the market's, so a ring you listed at 30 div came out at about 30 div. Your own listing is now left out, the card says so, and the log marks it *[your own listing]*. In 47 saved checks it was in 15 of the 16 items with a price note.
- **From contributors:** Where to spend works signed out of pathofexile.com: it shrinks its search step by step like Find upgrades does (every slot said "too many filters"), and when every slot fails the same way it shows when it ran, with *Search again now* (thanks Spardutti, #7).
- **Prices from listings like yours:** a price check reads each listing it finds and compares it with your item on the stats it's priced on. Listings with more than yours (a ring with your resistances and damage plus 80 life and attack speed) or much less are left out of the price, so better items no longer push yours up. If too few listings are just like yours, the price comes from the ones that differ by one stat, and the card says so; it's never "solid" then.
- **More listings before judging:** a price check now loosens its search until at least 5 are found (it stopped at 3, so a jewel came out at 5 div from asking prices of 2, 5 and 13 div). The looser matches that aren't like your item are left out again, so they add evidence without changing what's compared.
- **Cheap listings aren't thrown out as baits any more:** the old rule compared each cheap listing with the middle of the whole market. Most listings on the trade site are overpriced ones that haven't sold, so real offers at 30–300 ex for a ring could be dropped as "price-fixing baits", and a ~60 ex ring read 4 div. Now only one or two listings far under the next ones are baits (or a group a hundred times under them). The F3 card says how many were left out.
- **Waystone and PoE1 map price checks compare like with like:** a waystone's numbers (item rarity, pack size, monster rarity, effectiveness, drop chance) come from its mods, so it's now searched on its tier, corruption, mod count ("8 mods") and those numbers close to yours, loosening a little at a time; and it's priced only on the listings whose numbers are at least close to yours. Mods are counted as the trade site counts them (a two-line mod like "increased Critical Hit Chance" + "Critical Damage Bonus" is one), the search never goes far under your numbers (it widens the time listed and the mod count instead), and a waystone is never priced on weaker ones: if every listing found is weaker, it says it's worth more than their price instead of giving one. Before, cheap waystones with much weaker numbers set the price (an 8-mod corrupted T15 read 67 ex instead of several divines; another, counted as 9 mods, read 224 ex instead of ~3 div). PoE1 maps also use an Originator map's More Scarabs / Currency / Maps / Divination Cards, the kind of map quality, and whether they're blighted or blight-ravaged (a plain map is no longer compared with blighted ones).
- **Tablet price checks use every mod:** tablets no longer lose their gold, rare chest and other mods the trade site words differently ("(Gold Piles)", "an additional Rare Chest"), or mods without a number. "Unscalable Value" notes and the map device help lines are no longer read as mods.
- **Price checks explain themselves in the log:** each one logs the item, what it was searched on and what was left out (and why), every search level and how many it found, every listing price read (with baits, stale listings and listings not like yours marked, and how each differs from yours), and the verdict with its confidence. F3 logs poe.ninja answers and cached prices too. Each check is also kept in `price-checks.jsonl` next to the log (the item and every listing read, public trade data only), so prices can be replayed with `node tools/replay-prices.js` and tuned on real checks. Send the log with a price that looks wrong.
- **Tablets priced far too high:** a tablet found on all its mods, listed at 1 chaos, 2 chaos, 1 div, 1 div, 15 div and 200 chaos, came out at 8 div: the two cheap ones were taken for baits and the median landed between 1 div and 15 div. When the listings are known to be like yours (a tablet's first search, or the waystone and gear listings judged like yours), cheap ones only count as baits under a real market (three or more sellers close together), and only listings within 3x of the cheapest set the price, so far dearer asks don't lift it. It now says about 100 ex. `tools/replay-prices.js` also judges listing age as it was at the check, and treats tablets as the app does.
- **Tell it when a price is wrong:** the F3 card and *What's it worth?* have *Too low / About right / Too high*, and a box for what it's really worth ("3 div", "250 ex", "40 chaos"). Your answer is saved with that check, and `tools/replay-prices.js` scores the pricing against your answers (how far off, then and now), so every change to the price checker can be measured on real items. When few of the 40 cheapest listings are like your item, 40 more are read before judging.
- **Unique keyword mods show:** mods without a number on uniques ("Cannot be Ignited") were missing from the popup.
- **Map check:** "increased Armour, Evasion and Energy Shield" counts all three (it was read as Armour only), and "Physical Damage as Extra Chaos" no longer counts as all of their damage.
- **Find upgrades:** gear searches don't spend filter slots on weapon-only or armour-only stats that can't roll on the item (local attack speed on a ring). A budget says which listings it covers ("≤ 2 div (ex listings only)"): the trade site takes one currency per search.
- **Sign-in window:** only the sign-in pages of pathofexile.com's account providers open in it (not all of google.com or live.com); anything else is refused and logged.
- **Settings and character page:** typed guide skill levels survive the page redrawing; an anoint or rune check started before a character switch no longer shows up on the new character; buttons and spinners recover when a search fails.
- **Website price proxy:** only the poe.ninja lists the app reads are passed on.
- **Two-handed weapons judged without your offhand (PoE2):** staves, quarterstaves and talismans were judged with your focus or shield still counted, so they looked better than they are. Now every two-hander empties the offhand it can't be used with (Path of Building's own rules: a bow keeps its quiver, Giant's Blood and the like are respected). An offhand you couldn't hold with your two-hander says so instead of getting a verdict.
- **"Where to spend" no longer drops your offhand:** checking two items together with a two-hander in them took the offhand out of Path of Building's copy of your build for good, so every later verdict was off until a restart.
- **"Reduced" mods are searched:** mods the trade site only lists the way they roll ("40% reduced Freeze Duration on you", "15% reduced Mana Cost of Skills") were left out of searches and price checks. They're matched now, the right way round.
- **Price checks keep global mods on weapons and armour:** "Critical Hit Chance for Spells" on a wand or "35% of Armour also applies to Elemental Damage" on a body armour were mistaken for the item's own (local) numbers and left out of *What's it worth?*.
- **Jewels are compared with your weakest one** instead of always counting as a new jewel (and always "keep"), now that there's room for 40.
- **PoE1 maximum resistances count:** "+5% to maximum Fire Resistance" and "+1% to all maximum Resistances" raise your caps.
- **Imported weapons get their real kind (PoE2):** a Smithing Hammer or Morning Star from a Path of Building import was taken for a sceptre (and greataxes for maces); the app now asks Path of Building. Builds on weapon set II show that set as your weapon and offhand, and importing a new build takes off old imported items it doesn't have.
- **Safer with the trade site's limits:** the app now counts searches the site already saw (your browser, before the app started), and listing loads from several places at once go one at a time, so a burst of F3 presses can't get you rate-limited.
- **A search you replaced doesn't open your browser:** clicking Find upgrades on another slot (or changing game) while one was loading opened the first in your browser over the game.
- **Popup fixes:** a slow answer (a price, a search) no longer shows up on the next item; when the popup redraws, its "Copy price note" and "Open anyway" buttons keep working; a failed search no longer leaves the spinner going.
- **Settings fixes:** Find upgrades rebuilds its stats when you switch character or game (it could search with the last character's item); typing weights and pressing Tab keeps your place.
- **Path of Building engine:** uses about a fifth less memory after a few build loads; restarts by itself when it stops answering, instead of making everything after it wait; a crash while starting fails at once instead of after 90 seconds.
- **Faster:** the build file is read once instead of many times per item judged, a new item's F2 no longer writes the whole settings file before the popup shows, and the trade site's data is loaded once at start-up. The log keeps the previous one as `.log.1` when it gets big.

## 0.5.1 - 2026-10-26
- **New look, built like the games' own UI:** popups are item tooltips (a centred header tinted by the item's rarity, thin separators, grey labels with white values, mods in the games' blue), headings and buttons use real small caps (Alegreya Sans SC) instead of spaced capitals, and the gold corner ornaments, gradients and glow are gone. Better is shown in the games' blue and worse in red (colour-blind mode: light blue and orange). Item names on My gear show in their rarity colour. The website has the same look.
- **Prices in chaos orbs too (PoE2):** every price also says what it is in chaos, from poe.ninja's rate: the F3 popup, *What's it worth?*, poe.ninja prices and the trade results ("≈ 494 ex (0.7 div · 35 chaos)").
- **Where to spend: the chosen items are links.** Click an item's name (the best value or the biggest gain) to open it in the results window: that listing comes first, marked *You picked this*, with the rest of the slot's search under it. If it has sold since, the search is shown again and it says so.
- **Tablets read properly:** tablet wording ("… in Map", "… in your Maps") is read as the waystone's, so Path of Building works out the mods that act on you, and every tablet and waystone mod in the trade site's list now lands in a group, including a new **Map boss** group. The tablet card says what it adds and what its mods are for ("Adds an Otherworldy Breach: mods 2 for more loot, 1 for more or harder monsters"). More monster mods are worked out: critical damage, armour and evasion, less extra damage from your crits, energy shield on top of life.
- **Runes the copied text doesn't name:** the app works out which rune it is from its mod line, and otherwise shows what your rune gives now (Path of Building with the socket empty). Your own rune is never offered as an upgrade.
- **Loot filter mod highlights for every slot, both games:** weapons and offhands included, with the affix names your item's own base can roll (Path of Building's mod data), for magic and rare items. When your gear or build changes, a notification offers to make the filter again in one click.
- **Guide skills:** a *Skills* panel for guide builds (.build files have no gem levels, quality or main skill). Set every gem's level (for the stage, top level, or a number) and quality, or one gem apart; pick the main skill items are judged on and which other skills count in the total DPS. *Compare skills* has Path of Building work out each skill's damage as the main one and says which does the most. Your choices stay when the guide file updates, and a pasted .build can change stage or skills too (the app keeps a copy).
- **Best runes and soul cores (PoE2, Character page):** every rune, soul core and idol that fits each socket, tried by Path of Building with your priorities: the best for each socket, what all the picks do together, poe.ninja prices and the best value to buy first. Gear with no socket is tried with one added (Artificer's Orb); corrupted gear is left out; only tiers your level can use.
- **Waystones, maps and tablets:** monster mods are now worked out by Path of Building on its enemy (extra damage, penetration, crits, attack and cast speed, accuracy): how much smaller the biggest hit you survive gets and how much less long you last. Tougher monsters (resistances, armour, more life) show what they do to your damage and kill time. Mods come in groups (acting on you, monsters hit harder, tougher monsters, more monsters, loot, league content) with the waystone's rewards. F2 on a **precursor tablet** now gets the same card, with its uses left, instead of only a price.
- **Where to spend:** a Jewel row when your tree has jewel sockets, with which socket the best jewel goes in (empty sockets counted first).
- **Loot filter:** highlights the uniques your guide uses that you don't have yet, the best runes from the rune check, and better bases of the kinds you wear (Path of Building's base data, usable now or within 5 levels). While levelling, bases show from your level. The app remembers the filter it made for each character, says when your gear or build has changed since, and **Remake** writes it again in place, with your own filter read again.
- **More than 6 jewel sockets:** builds with lots of jewels (cluster jewels, big trees) keep all of them, up to 40. A Path of Building import brings every socketed jewel, and *My gear* shows your jewel slots plus one empty one to add the next.
- **Tidier Settings:** six cards in three even columns. The results-window hotkey and the clipboard option sit with the other hotkeys, popup size and detail with the popup, and startup with updates. The usage-statistics details fold away under *What's sent*.
- **Guide builds without a PoB link:** link a guide's Build Planner file (**.build**, from Mobalytics, Maxroll and others; Documents\My Games\Path of Exile 2\BuildPlanner) and the app turns it into a Path of Building build:
  - the passive tree from Grinding Gear Games' own data (every passive placed), weapon-set passives included;
  - the skills with their supports, at gem levels that fit the stage;
  - the guide's uniques by name, and rares it spells out, but only in slots you haven't copied your own item into;
  - **levelling guides:** pick the stage (Levels 1–30, 31–60, …) or *Follow my level*, which moves with your levelling level;
  - "+5 to any attribute" passives follow the colours of the build's gems, and the import says what it couldn't place;
  - the file is followed like a PoB build file, so a newer version of the guide updates your character.
- **Movement speed counts:** Path of Building's movement speed is part of the verdict. Each point gained or lost moves the score, and losing 3% or more is never a clean upgrade (*Priorities → Keep my movement speed*). The popup shows "Speed 35% → 25%", and *Find upgrades* for boots only shows boots about as fast as yours.
- **Verdicts name the skill** the damage was judged on ("DPS (Spark)"), and show Runic Ward changes.
- **Where to spend remembers:** each character's last two runs are kept for a week, so they're still there after a restart. The page says how old the run is, which slots you've changed since, and which slots are better or worse value than last time. *See all* searches again when the run is old.
- **Price check fixes, from comparisons with PoE Overlay II:**
  - a wide market no longer makes real cheap listings look like price-fixing baits (a robe was priced at 55 div with comparable ones listed from 15);
  - the closest search with a few listings is used; it no longer widens until it finds cheap, different items (a sceptre came out at 5 div when the only comparable ones were listed at 82–100 div);
  - "Allies in your Presence deal …% increased Damage" is always kept on sceptres.
- **Fix:** items copied with older versions of the app are now used by Path of Building again (they were judged against an empty slot).
- **Licence:** the app is now free software under the GNU GPL v3 (LICENSE and NOTICE.md). Forks are welcome as long as they stay open source and use their own name and update channel.
- **How sure is the price?** Trade-site prices now say *solid price*, *rough price* or *a guess* (hover for why: few sellers, prices far apart, or looser matches), in the popup, on F3 and in the results window.
- **List it at… for rares, waystones and tablets too:** when the price isn't a guess, *What's it worth?* suggests a price just under the cheapest comparable listing, with *Copy price note* for your premium stash tab.
- **F3 remembers:** pressing F3 on the same item within an hour answers at once from the last check ("checked 12 min ago"), without another trade search; *refresh* asks again.
- **Faster first check:** the trade site's stat list is read at start-up, so the first F2 or F3 doesn't wait for it.
- **Price-only hotkey (F3):** hover an item and press F3 for a small popup with just its price: poe.ninja's for currency, gems, uniques and the rest it lists, the trade site's for rare and magic gear, waystones, maps and tablets. F2 still does the full check. Change it (to Alt+E, say) or turn it off in Settings → Hotkeys.
- **Waystones, maps and Precursor Tablets can be price checked:**
  - Waystones and maps: *What's it worth?* on their card searches the same tier, rarity and corruption with similar numbers (item rarity, pack size, waystone drop chance, monster rarity; quantity, rarity and pack size on PoE1 maps). Their monster mods don't count, since buyers price on the numbers.
  - Tablets: their kind (Expedition, Delirium…), uses left and mods.
- **What's it worth? is much closer to what items sell for.** Rare and magic gear used to be compared only with items at least as good on every single line, so the few that matched were the dear end of the market (an item that sells for about 20 div could show hundreds). Now:
  - it searches the way buyers do: total resistances, life, attributes and movement speed, the item's defences or a weapon's DPS, and its other worthwhile mods, each within a range of your values; filler mods (light radius, stun threshold, thorns…) no longer narrow the search;
  - movement speed, +skill levels and Spirit are always kept, so boots aren't priced against boots without movement speed;
  - only recent listings from online sellers count (the last 3 days, widening to a week and then 2 weeks if there are too few), so items that have sat unsold for weeks don't set the price;
  - the price is the middle of the cheapest five sellers (it was the cheapest eight).
- **Your build stays in sync:**
  - A pobb.in link is remembered for the character: *Update now* reads it again.
  - Or link the build file Path of Building saves (*Or link your PoB build file…*): each time you save in PoB, the app reads it again by itself. Only the file you pick is read.
- **Best anoint / instil** on the Character page: Path of Building tries every notable that can go on your amulet and lists the best for your build, with what each does to your damage and effective HP and the oils (PoE1) or distilled emotions (PoE2) it takes.
- **Mod tiers:** *More* in the popup lists the item's mods with their tier (T1, T2…) and how high each rolled, and results show tiers too. New installs copy items with advanced details (the game's Ctrl+Alt+C) so tiers are there from the start.
- **Price by chosen stats:** one *Total elemental resistance* row (the trade site's own total, so fire, cold, lightning and all-res mods count alike), and 80% / 90% / 100% buttons to set every minimum at once.
- **Results:** *One per seller* shows only each seller's best listing.
- **A cleaner popup:**
  - It says CHECKING… until Path of Building answers, instead of showing a gear-score verdict that then changes.
  - A resistance or damage loss is said once, in one line under the badge.
  - Changes of zero are left out.
  - The poe.ninja price reads "≈ 9 div · worth selling".
  - *What's it worth?* answers with a big number, the range and listing count under it.
  - The other searches are grouped under "More searches".
  - Primary buttons are gold, so red only ever means a loss; colour-blind mode now covers banners and borders too.
  - Nothing is smaller than 12 px.
  - At Extra large size the popup is never cut off.
  - When the trade site doesn't answer, the popup offers a button instead of opening your browser over the game.
- **Results window:** only the controls stay pinned while you scroll. Sellers who are online come first (offline ones are dimmed). The listings with the best value and the biggest upgrade are labelled.
- **My gear:**
  - One line per item (click it for all its mods), and Clear asks once more.
  - The resistance boxes hide when Path of Building is loaded, since it knows your resistances.
  - The setup guide notices a loaded build and ends by waiting for your first F2: "✓ It works".
- **Find upgrades:**
  - The filter bar no longer covers the panels under it.
  - Waystones and maps only offer a price check.
  - Saved searches, alerts and recent searches are one Watchlist with two tabs.
- **Other pages:** Build & stats folds the gear-score tools away when Path of Building is loaded. Settings has an Appearance group and an Advanced section. Where to spend shows one message when every slot fails the same way.
- **Safer app:** its windows can't be turned into websites (a link dropped on a window does nothing), only its own pages can talk to it, and the pathofexile.com sign-in window stays on pathofexile.com with no browser permissions. F2 only presses Ctrl+C when the game is the active window, and the log never contains what you copied. A crafted Path of Building code can no longer freeze the app. Without signing in, the app no longer retries a refused trade search through a hidden browser page.
- **Fixes:**
  - Uniques with several versions imported from Path of Building only keep the version they are (a Kaom's Heart no longer counts both versions' life).
  - The gem check finds support gems ("Impale Support").
  - *Where to spend* results belong to the character they were made for.
  - Unidentified items get no verdict ("identify it, then press the hotkey again").
  - Uniques with several versions are priced as the version they are; when the app can't tell, it says so and doesn't suggest a listing price.
  - Dual wielders: a one-hand weapon is also compared with the weapon in your other hand.
  - The results window keeps acting on the search it shows while a new one loads.
  - A unique's poe.ninja price stays after changing a setting.
  - *Judge with Path of Building* is set separately for each game.
  - Requests that get no answer give up after 20 seconds instead of spinning forever.
- **Security: updated to Electron 44** (Chromium 152, Node 24). The app was on Electron 33, which stopped getting security fixes in April 2025; this matters because the app opens pathofexile.com's trade and sign-in pages in its own windows. The clipboard handling was reworked for Electron 44, which reads and writes the clipboard asynchronously.
- **Newer build tools:** electron-updater 6.8, electron-builder 26, Node 24 and the latest GitHub Actions for the release builds.
- **From contributors:** Find upgrades counts "+#% to all Elemental Resistances" and "+# to all Attributes" mods toward the resistances and attributes you search for (thanks Spardutti, #3).
- **From contributors:** The popup's × closes it, and hovering the item's name keeps it open (thanks Spardutti, #4).

## 0.5.0 - 2026-10-25
- **From contributors:** Gear score: resistance is capped on the total you still need, not on each line, and runes take resistance after mods (thanks Spardutti, #1).
- **Corrupted items:** trade results mark corrupted listings in red next to the item level, the results window has *Hide corrupted*, and Find upgrades has *Skip corrupted items*, which leaves them out of every upgrade search (Find upgrades, Where to spend, alerts). Price checks still match the item's own corruption.
- **Damage per hit:** item comparisons show how your main skill's average hit changes ("Hit 310,969 → 305,687", your minions' for a minion build) next to the DPS and effective HP change, and the Character page shows it as *Damage per hit*. Dual-wield builds now get it (and crit and hit chance) too.
- **Move the popup:** drag it by its name and it stays there for the next items (Settings → Popup → Position: *Where I dragged it*; pick another position to go back).
- **PoE2 instilled amulets:** like PoE1 anoints, an amulet that doesn't have your instilled passive now says so ("Your amulet's instilled passive (Unbending) isn't on this one… Instilling it again costs Distilled Emotions"). Path of Building already counted the instill in the verdict.
- **Travel to hideout on every instant buyout:** the button was only there when the listing came with a hideout token, which the trade site only gives a signed-in account, so it rarely showed. Now every instant-buyout listing in the results has it; the first time you sign in to pathofexile.com, and the app asks the trade site for that one listing's token when you click.
- **Gem check:** F2 on a gem your build uses and Path of Building tries it at this gem's level and quality in your build: UPGRADE / NO CHANGE / WORSE with the DPS and effective HP change, for every skill group that uses it. A better gem than yours is a KEEP.
- **Selling helper:** price cards and uniques suggest a listing price a little under poe.ninja (in divines when it's worth one or more; for the whole stack) and copy a `~price` note to paste into the item's note in a premium stash tab.
- **Search history:** Find upgrades keeps your last 30 trade searches for each game: run one again with one click, or ☆ Save it to get alerts.
- **Boss check (PoE1):** the Character page works out how many of each Path of Building boss preset hit (Shaper, Sirus, Exarch, Eater, Maven…, at Pinnacle and Uber) you can take. Your own Path of Building configuration is left as it was.
- **Search strings:** the Character page builds a search to paste at vendors and in your stash (movement speed, resistances you're short of, life, + gem levels, three linked sockets in PoE1) and one for the map device that leaves out maps with the mods you marked dangerous, with a count against the game's 50-letter limit. The map card copies the map one too.
- **Leveling mode:** Find upgrades → *Leveling: only items I can wear at level N* (or your Path of Building character's level) adds a level requirement to every upgrade search.
- **PoE1 3.29:** the map check knows Thorns (rare monsters with Physical or Elemental Thorns, which replaced reflect) and checks them against your main skill. Socket colours aren't checked any more, since any gem now fits any socket.
- **Path of Building's score is shown as points** ("+6.3 pts for your build") instead of a %: it was never a plain percentage (damage and effective HP changes, weighted, plus half a point per resistance point toward your cap), and the tooltip says so.
- **Clearer for PoE1:** no gear-score wording or profiles in PoE1 (My gear, Build & stats, the first-run guide), PoE1 items without a Path of Building build say NOT JUDGED instead of "empty slot", and maps without a build say NOT CHECKED instead of "looks fine".
- **When poe.ninja can't be reached** the price card says so, instead of "no price".
- **Rare resistance drops are flagged** when the app doesn't know your resistance totals (UPGRADE, BUT… with a note to type them in).
- **Keep, sell or vendor:** under the verdict, one plain answer: KEEP (an upgrade for you), SELL (not an upgrade but worth something, from poe.ninja or your own *What's it worth?*), VENDOR, or PRICE CHECK for a rare with no price yet.
- **Build-value alerts:** on *Where to spend*, *Alert me…* on any slot: "tell me when a new Gloves listing is +10% for my build under 2 div". New listings are checked with your build and only the ones that clear it are notified.
- **Price history:** the price card draws poe.ninja's last 7 days, and uniques get a small line next to their price.
- **Faster prices and Where to spend:** every poe.ninja list for a game comes in one request (gathered by poe2upgrade.com's cache); Where to spend shows each slot as soon as it's searched and keeps its answer for 30 minutes; listing fetches slow down by themselves near the trade site's limit; Path of Building starts in the background when the app opens, so the first check is quicker.

A third ring slot (The Unseen Hand) is tried when your build has one, and Limited-to jewels you already have are only compared with themselves.

Off with Settings → *Send anonymous usage statistics*.

## 0.4.3 - 2026-10-23
