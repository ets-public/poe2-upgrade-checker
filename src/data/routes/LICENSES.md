# Route data: sources and licences

The campaign routes in this folder are built from two open-source projects, used under the MIT
licence. Thank you to their authors.

- `poe1.json`: the route and area ids come from **exile-leveling** by HeartofPhos
  (https://github.com/HeartofPhos/exile-leveling), league-start route with all three bandits killed.
  Copyright (c) 2025 HeartofPhos.
- `poe2.json`: the route, area ids, level targets and permanent rewards come from **Splitstone**
  (https://github.com/noman-omen/poe2-splitstone), patch 0.5.5 (Forbidden Rites).
  Copyright (c) 2026 Splitstone contributors.

The "endgame" cards and the reward notes added to the steps are this app's own.
