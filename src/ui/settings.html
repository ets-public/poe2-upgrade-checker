Your build in Path of Building

Paste your Path of Building export code (Import/Export Build → Generate: Path of Building 2 for PoE2, Path of Building Community for PoE1) or a pobb.in link. Following a guide without a PoB link? Link its Build Planner file (.build) below. The app then judges every item with Path of Building's own calculations: what it does to your damage (or your minions'), effective HP, resistances and Spiritmana and life reservation. Make sure PoB's gear, tree, skills and Configuration match how you really play. Items you copy in game (F2) replace PoB's, so it always uses what you're wearing.

Loot filter for this build

Highlights drops worth a look: the uniques your guide uses that you don't have yet, the best runes for your sockets (after a check on the Character page), better bases of the kinds you wear, your own bases, and identified magic and rare items with the mods your build values most (weapons and offhands too). While levelling, bases show from your level. Pick your current filter and the app saves a copy with this section in front (your filter itself isn't changed). Then choose the new file in the game's options.

Custom search

Ignored stats

Stats you don't care about. Ignore leaves them out of the gear score and every search. Exclude also hides listings that have them.

Watchlist

Hotkeys

Check item Price only Results window
Hover an item in game and press a hotkey; the app presses Ctrl+C for you (once per press). Use a key the game doesn’t: F-keys work best (with WASD, D walks your character).

Trade site account optional

Signing in to pathofexile.com lets you Travel to hideout from the results and makes searches fuller. Your password goes only to pathofexile.com.

Usage statistics

What’s sent One small message to poe2upgrade.com when the app starts and every few hours: a random install number (made on this PC), the app version, Windows or Linux, beta or not, and which game you used that day. No account, character, items, builds or searches, and the site doesn't keep IP addresses.

Startup & updates

Betas get new features a little earlier and can have bugs. If something breaks, send the log to support@poe2upgrade.com.

Troubleshooting

Test popup shows here but not in game? Set the game to Windowed Fullscreen (Options → Graphics). Hotkey does nothing? Open the log. Your data is in %APPDATA%\PoE2 Upgrade Checker.
